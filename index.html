My Journey

From Rwanda to India — A Chapter of Growth

My path started in Rwanda, where I trained in networking hardware and internet technology — LAN setups, CCTV installation, and Windows Server environments — through three internships at NOVA SERVICES LTD in Kigali. That hands-on foundation shaped how I think about systems: start with what's physically connected before reasoning about what runs on top.

In 2025, I moved to Telangana, India to begin my BCA at SR University, expanding from networking into programming, cloud infrastructure, and AI. Alongside my coursework, I founded INGRA DIGITAL HUB to write about what I was learning, and joined CS Girlies as a workshop leader — turning my own progress into something other women could use too.
